# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owners

### Role Summary
Product Owners turn product direction into a well-refined backlog and help the delivery team clarify what needs to be built. The role may be filled by a Product Manager or a delegated teammate, depending on the project.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and refine the backlog
- Clarify acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Coordinate day-to-day prioritization with the delivery team
- Remove requirement ambiguity and help sequence work
- Validate completed work against acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Keep work ready, understandable, and prioritized
- Improve feedback speed between product decisions and delivery
- Reduce rework caused by unclear requirements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Manager on vision, outcomes, and prioritization trade-offs
- Partners with Developers to clarify scope and acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on delivery dependencies and sequencing
- Supports QA/Testing by defining testable acceptance criteria
- Engages Stakeholders to gather feedback and confirm priorities

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and planning sessions
- Acceptance criteria reviews
- Sprint updates and demos

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and design decisions while keeping delivery, maintainability, and team capabilities in view.

### Responsibilities
- Establish and communicate technical direction and standards
- Lead or review significant design decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and trade-offs
- Promote implementation approaches that support testability and reliability
- Mentor Developers and coordinate technical problem-solving

### Goals
- Create coherent, maintainable technical solutions
- Surface architectural risks early
- Improve technical consistency and delivery confidence

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on design, implementation, and code quality
- Coordinates technical constraints and risks with the Project Manager
- Aligns technical options and trade-offs with the Product Manager
- Partners with QA/Testing on testability and quality approaches
- Consults Stakeholders or dependent teams when decisions affect integrations or shared systems

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews
- Architecture and dependency discussions
- Engineering risk updates and planning sessions

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers represent user needs in product decisions, translating research and usability goals into clear designs and validated experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Understand user needs, behaviors, and accessibility requirements
- Create and iterate UX flows, wireframes, or prototypes
- Plan and conduct usability research or testing
- Validate designs with users and relevant stakeholders
- Provide implementation guidance to the delivery team

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer value
- Reduce the risk of building experiences that do not meet user needs
- Ensure accessibility and consistency are considered during delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with the Product Manager and Product Owner on problem framing and priorities
- Works with Developers on feasible implementation and design handoffs
- Coordinates usability acceptance and test scenarios with QA/Testing
- Shares research findings and design risks with the Project Manager and Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Discovery and user research sessions
- Design reviews and usability testing
- Handoff materials and design critiques

---

## QA/Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Test Leads define quality coverage and coordinate acceptance validation across the team. They may be formalized as a dedicated role or shared among QA specialists and Developers.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the test strategy
- Coordinate unit, integration, end-to-end, and regression testing
- Validate acceptance criteria and feature readiness
- Report quality risks, defects, and release blockers
- Promote testability and continuous quality practices

### Goals
- Provide confidence that changes meet acceptance criteria
- Detect risks and defects early
- Maintain balanced, efficient test coverage

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Manager and Product Owner on acceptance expectations
- Partners with Developers on test design and defect resolution
- Coordinates release readiness and quality risks with the Project Manager
- Collaborates with the Technical Lead on integration and test architecture
- Communicates release concerns to Stakeholders when needed

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and defect triage
- Quality readiness reports
- Release and smoke-test reviews

---

## Security/Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security/Privacy Leads ensure that security, compliance, and privacy requirements are understood and incorporated into project decisions and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements and risks
- Review proposed controls, threat models, and data handling
- Coordinate required scans, assessments, and approvals
- Advise on secure implementation and incident readiness
- Support escalation of unresolved security or privacy risks

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy exposure
- Make required controls observable and enforceable
- Support safe, compliant releases

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advises the Product Manager and Product Owner on risk and compliance constraints
- Partners with Developers and the Technical Lead on secure design and implementation
- Works with QA/Test Leads on security testing and verification
- Coordinates mitigations and escalations with the Project Manager
- Keeps affected Stakeholders informed of significant requirements or incidents

### Typical Communication
- Threat modeling and privacy reviews
- Security risk and remediation updates
- Release readiness and incident escalation

---

## Delivery / Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery / Engineering Managers support team capacity, engineering health, and effective execution. They complement the Project Manager's cross-project coordination rather than duplicating it.

### Responsibilities
- Support staffing and sustainable team capacity
- Improve engineering effectiveness, predictability, and process health
- Remove organizational or team-level delivery impediments
- Coordinate engineering dependencies and priorities
- Support career growth and team resilience

### Goals
- Improve sustainable delivery performance
- Strengthen team health and engineering capability
- Reduce recurring organizational impediments

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager on capacity, schedules, dependencies, and escalations
- Aligns team constraints and engineering priorities with the Product Manager
- Supports Developers by resolving team-level impediments and resourcing needs
- Coordinates technical execution with the Technical Lead
- Shares delivery health and quality trends with QA/Testing and Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Capacity and resource planning
- Engineering health reviews
- Delivery risk and dependency escalations

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams may combine roles or assign them to one or more people; the definitions clarify accountability without requiring every role to be staffed separately.
